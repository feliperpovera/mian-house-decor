Custom drapes made for your windows, not a catalogue size

Ready-made curtains rarely fit Miami windows: tall living-room glass, arched windows, sliding doors and wide patio openings. We measure each opening and sew the drapery to size in our own workshop, so it hangs straight, clears the floor the way you want and closes properly. Then we install it.

Drapery styles we make

The heading — how the fabric is gathered at the top — sets the look of a drape more than anything else. These are the styles we make most often in Miami homes.

Euro pleat drapes

Euro pleats are gathered and tacked right at the top edge, so each pleat fans out into soft, even folds below. The result is tailored but relaxed, and it suits contemporary rooms, condos and floor-to-ceiling glass where you want clean lines.

Pinch pleat and pleated drapes

Classic pinch pleats are stitched a few inches below the top, giving crisp, structured folds and a fuller drape. They work well in traditional and transitional rooms, dining rooms and bedrooms, and on large living-room windows.

Sheer drapes

Sheers soften glare and add daytime privacy while letting light through — a good fit for high-rise windows and sliding doors. They can be layered with a lined or blackout drape on a second track for full control.

Blackout and lined drapes

Blackout-lined drapery darkens bedrooms and media rooms. A standard lining protects the face fabric from Miami sun, improves privacy and helps the drape hang with more body.

Outdoor drapes

For covered patios, terraces and balconies we sew drapes in outdoor-rated fabrics that handle sun and humidity — often paired with reupholstered outdoor cushions in a coordinating fabric.

Fabrics and lining for Miami light

Light, heat and humidity matter here. We help you choose for each room:

  • Sheers to soften glare without darkening the room
  • Lined drapes to protect fabric from fading and add privacy in sunny rooms
  • Blackout lining for bedrooms, nurseries and media rooms
  • Outdoor-rated textiles for patios, terraces and humid spaces
  • Coordinating cushions and pillows sewn in the same fabric as your drapes

We bring samples to your home so you can see each option against your own windows, walls and furniture before anything is cut.

Frequently asked questions

How much do custom drapes cost in Miami?

It depends on window size, fabric, lining and heading style. Send us photos of your windows and we will send a free quote within 24 hours.

What is the difference between euro pleat and pinch pleat drapes?

Euro pleats are tacked at the very top edge and fan out into soft folds, for a cleaner, more contemporary look. Pinch pleats are stitched a few inches below the top, giving crisper, more structured folds and a more traditional look.

Do you measure and install the drapes?

Yes. We measure each opening so the drapery is sewn to fit, and we install it in your home.

Should my curtains be lined?

In Miami, usually yes for sunny rooms: lining protects the fabric from fading and improves privacy. Sheers are the exception, when you want light to pass through.

Can you make blackout curtains?

Yes. Blackout-lined drapery works well for bedrooms and media rooms.

Do you make outdoor curtains?

Yes, in outdoor-rated fabrics for covered patios and terraces.

Can you make cushions or pillows to match my drapes?

Yes. We sew coordinating cushions and pillows in the same fabric as your drapery.

Which areas do you serve?

All of Miami-Dade, including Brickell, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Kendall, Doral and North Miami.
